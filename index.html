Why This Exists
"I watched my dad give everything to a company that discarded him before he could enjoy it. That's not my story. And it doesn't have to be yours."

The Corporate Escapee started as a personal mission — to help people build a life they actually want before it's too late. Six years ago, I left corporate without a clear plan. What I discovered was that the skills, relationships, and experience I'd spent 20 years building were worth more on the open market than they ever paid me in a salary.

The problem was never the talent. It's the belief that there's another way. This community exists to close that gap — one escapee at a time.

Brett Trainor · Founder, The Corporate Escapee 🐬✌️
