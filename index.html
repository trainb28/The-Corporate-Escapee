What We Believe

Real Human Connection
Still Matters Most.

The world is racing toward digital and AI. We're not against any of it. We use the tools. We build with them. But the thing that actually moves us forward, the thing that won't be replaced, is people who get it talking to people who get it.

Why GenX
We Remember Both Sides

We were the last generation that knew how to focus by default. Outside until the streetlights came on. Then corporate trained it out of us, and AI accelerated everything. We sit on both sides of the bridge.

Why Now
The Window Is Real

Corporate is changing fast. AI is reshaping the work. The people who win the next decade are the ones who build a life-first future on their own terms, with peers who can move with them.

Why Together
Human-to-Human Wins

In an AI-augmented world, real conversations are the differentiator. Other GenXers who've been where you are. Conversations that don't happen anywhere else. That's the room we're building.

Why This Exists
"I watched my dad give everything to a company that discarded him before he could enjoy it. That's not my story. And it doesn't have to be yours."

The Corporate Escapee started as a personal mission. To help people build a life they actually want before it's too late. Six years ago, I left corporate without a clear plan. What I discovered was that the skills, relationships, and experience I'd spent 20 years building were worth more on the open market than they ever paid me in a salary.

The problem was never the talent. It's the belief that there's another way. This community exists to close that gap, one escapee at a time.

Brett Trainor · Founder, The Corporate Escapee
